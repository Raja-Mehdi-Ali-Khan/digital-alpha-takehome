# Assumptions

## Product / Business
- Single-user application for the scope of this take-home.
- Coin earning rule: 1 coin per ₹100 spent, only on SUCCESS status, capped at 50 coins per transaction.
- Rewards catalogue is static (6 items) for this assignment.
- Currency is always INR.

## Technical
- PostgreSQL 16 is acceptable (Docker image used).
- Frontend will talk to backend on http://localhost:8000 during development.
